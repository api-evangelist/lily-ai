- [Google just told the market AI content isn't the moat. For retailers, the moat isn't AI-optimized content. It's structured, machine-readable product data.
- [$80M in incremental revenue last month, one optimization: the feed](https://lily-marketing.web.app/blog/80m-incremental-revenue-one-optimization-the-feed): Four 28-day Google Ads tests across a luxury house, a US department store, a global home retailer, and an athletic-wear brand. - [Performance Max for large catalogs, without the guesswork](https://lily-marketing.web.app/blog/google-ads-pmax-for-large-catalogs): PMax can be a black box. We break down how catalog-aware product intelligence makes it work for retailers with tens of thousands of SKUs.
- [Stripe is building the rails. Stripe is building the payment rails. The brands that win on them will already have their product data structured for agents.
